3 · How the lens power is actually calculated

This is the exact method used in your textbook: power of a lens P = 1/f, where f is the focal length in metres. A concave lens has negative power, a convex lens has positive power.

Teaching model: the ray diagram inside the eye is schematic (not to scale) so it fits on screen, but every dioptre value shown — for the defect and for the lens — is calculated with the real formula P = 1/f used in the CBSE/NCERT Class 10 syllabus (Chapter: The Human Eye and the Colourful World).
